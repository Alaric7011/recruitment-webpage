Culture Fit

Who Thrives Here

We believe radical honesty builds better teams. Here's who fits and who doesn't.

People Who Thrive Here ✦

  • Builders — people who create things, not just plan them.
  • Self-starters — who move forward without being told twice.
  • Fast learners — who pick up new skills in days, not months.
  • Curious minds — who ask "why?" before asking "how?".
  • Problem solvers — who see constraints as puzzles, not blockers.
  • Action-oriented — done is better than perfect, always.

Probably Not The Best Fit ✦

  • People who need constant supervision and hand-holding.
  • Those who prefer slow execution over fast iteration.
  • Low ownership mindset — "that's not my job" culture.
  • Fear of ambiguity — we don't always have the perfect playbook.
  • People optimizing for title over impact and learning.

How We Hire

The Hiring Process

We respect your time. Our process is short, honest, and moves fast.

Step 01

Apply

Fill out our short application form. No cover letters needed. We care about your energy, hunger, and curiosity — not formatting.

Step 02

HR Interview

A short conversation with our HR team to understand your journey, communication style, and role fit. Relaxed, thoughtful, and useful for both sides.

Step 03

Founder Conversation

A 30-minute call with Gaurav or the core team. Low pressure, high signal. Ask us anything about TSS, the mission, or the role.

Step 04

Final Decision

We move fast. You'll hear from us within 48–72 hours of the conversation.
